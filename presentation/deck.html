Key design assumptions
  • – Maya is the first line of defense and reviews every analysis.
  • – Derek can open any analysis, but his job is resolving escalations.
  • – The system recommends; humans keep decision authority.
  • – Information is disclosed progressively, by responsibility.
  • – Every decision and escalation reason stays traceable.
!Working assumption

Near limit → escalate. If the value is within spec but its uncertainty range crosses a limit, Maya sends it to Derek.

e.g. #201 porosity 35.6%, range 35.2–36.1% vs. spec 32–36%. SirenOpt: reasonable assumptions are fine; the assessment doesn't depend on them.
